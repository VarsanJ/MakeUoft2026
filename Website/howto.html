❓ How To Use the Relief Jacket

User manual and safety guide (prototype)

1) What is the Relief Jacket?

The Relief Jacket is a wearable emergency support device designed for use during natural disasters such as earthquakes, floods, hurricanes, or fires. It helps users:

  • Send SOS (distress) signals
  • Share their location with nearby users and rescue teams
  • View nearby safe houses and shelters on a map
  • Monitor body/environment temperature

2) Powering On the Jacket

  1. Make sure the jacket battery is charged.
  2. Press and hold the power button on the jacket for 2 seconds.
  3. Wait for the LED indicator to turn solid blue (means powered on).
  4. If the LED blinks red, the battery is low — recharge before use.

Tip: Always charge the jacket fully before going outdoors in disaster-prone areas.

3) Using the Joystick Controls

The jacket has a small joystick that lets you navigate the website functions:

  • Up: Open Map
  • Down: Open Temperature page
  • Left: Go back to Home
  • Right: Open How-To page
  • Press (click): Send SOS signal

You can also use the buttons on this website if you are accessing it from a phone or laptop.

4) Sending an SOS (Emergency Mode)

Use SOS only in real emergencies.

  1. Press the joystick button firmly for 2 seconds.
  2. The jacket will vibrate and flash red to confirm SOS is active.
  3. Your location will be shared with:
    • Nearby Relief Jacket users
    • Rescue teams (when connected to the network)
  4. The SOS page on the website will show your distress status.

To cancel SOS, press and hold the joystick again for 3 seconds.

5) Using the Map

The Map page helps you navigate to safety:

  • Blue marker: Your current location
  • Green markers: Safe houses / shelters
  • Red markers: Users who have sent SOS

Zoom in/out on the map to see nearby safe locations and people in need.

6) Checking Temperature

The Temperature page shows readings from the jacket sensor:

  • Normal range: 36°C – 37.5°C (body temperature)
  • Low temperature warning: Risk of hypothermia
  • High temperature warning: Risk of fever or heat stress

If abnormal temperatures are detected, seek medical attention when possible.

7) Battery & Charging

  • Charge the jacket using the provided USB cable.
  • Charging time: ~2 hours for full charge.
  • Battery life: ~8 hours of active use.
  • Avoid exposing charging port to water.

8) Safety & Care

  • Do not submerge the electronics in water.
  • Do not open the electronic module.
  • Store in a dry place when not in use.
  • Clean jacket fabric separately from electronics.

9) Troubleshooting

  • Jacket not turning on: Charge battery.
  • Map not showing: Check internet connection.
  • Location not updating: Enable location permission in browser.
  • SOS not sending: Ensure device is connected to the network.

10) Important Disclaimer

This project is a prototype created for a hackathon and is not a certified medical or emergency response device. In real emergencies, always contact official emergency services when available.
